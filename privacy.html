Privacy Policy

App: video-uploader  |  Last updated: 27 November 2026

Who uses this app

video-uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account (Curiouschhorichhora). It is not offered to the public and no other people log in to it.

What data is used

The app uses only the access token, Page ID and Instagram account ID of the owner's own accounts, and the video files, titles and captions the owner chooses to publish. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers, followers or any other person.

Where data is kept

Tokens are stored only on the owner's private server, readable only by the owner. They are never shared with third parties. Video files are deleted from the server after they are published.

Data deletion

No data of other users is stored, so there is nothing to delete for them. The owner can remove all app data at any time by deleting the tokens from the server and removing the app in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations. Questions or deletion requests: contact the owner through the Facebook Page Curiouschhorichhora.
