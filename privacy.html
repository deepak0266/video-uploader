Privacy Policy

App: video-uploader  |  Last updated: 27 November 2026

Who uses this app

video-uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (Curious Chhori Chhora), Facebook Page and Instagram account (Curiouschhorichhora). It is not offered to the public and no other people log in to it.

What data is used

The app uses only the access tokens, channel/Page IDs and Instagram account ID of the owner's own accounts, and the video files, thumbnails, titles and captions the owner chooses to publish. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers, followers, subscribers or any other person.

YouTube / Google data

The app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API only for the owner's own YouTube channel: to upload the owner's videos, set their thumbnails, and read the owner's own channel and video statistics (such as views and watch time) for the owner's private report. No data of other YouTube users is accessed. The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's private server and is never shared.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Facebook / Instagram data

The app uses the Meta Graph API only for the owner's own Facebook Page and its linked Instagram account: to publish the owner's videos and Reels with their captions and cover images, and to check publishing status and limits. No data of other Facebook or Instagram users is accessed.

Where data is kept

Tokens are stored only on the owner's private server, readable only by the owner, and are never shared with third parties. Video files and thumbnails are deleted from the server after they are published.

Data deletion

No data of other users is stored, so there is nothing to delete for them. The owner can remove all app data at any time by deleting the tokens from the server, removing Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and removing the app in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact the owner through the Facebook Page Curiouschhorichhora or the YouTube channel Curious Chhori Chhora.
